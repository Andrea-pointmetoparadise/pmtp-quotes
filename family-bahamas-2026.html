The Royal at Atlantis

Atlantis Paradise Island · Nassau, Bahamas · 5 Nights

The Royal is the tower that puts you at the center of everything Atlantis does well. Aquaventure waterpark — the one with the Leap of Faith body slide — is a 90-second walk from the lobby. The marine habitat with sharks and rays is free to wander. There's a proper beach on both sides of the property, a casino if the kids are asleep, and enough pools that you rarely wait. Harbor View rooms sit on the Nassau side: you watch ships and the old fort skyline, and the sun doesn't bake you out of bed before 8 AM.
