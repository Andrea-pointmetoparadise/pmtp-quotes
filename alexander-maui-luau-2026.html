Old Lāhainā Lūʻau
Alexander Family Graduation Trip

July 21, 2026  ·  Lāhainā, Maui, Hawaiʻi  ·  6 Adults

The Old Lāhainā Lūʻau has run nightly on the waterfront since 1974 — the longest-running luau on Maui, and still the most credible one. Dinner is served from an imu pit (underground oven), the bar is open all evening, and the hula is performed by dancers who compete internationally. This is the graduation evening the Alexanders will still be talking about at the next one.
