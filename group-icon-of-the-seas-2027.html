Neighborhoods
Thrill Island
Category 6 — the largest waterpark at sea — with six record-breaking slides including the Pressure Drop (first open free-fall slide at sea) and Frightening Bolt (tallest drop slide at sea). Plus the Crown's Edge skywalk, FlowRider surf simulator, mini golf, and rock climbing.
Chill Island
Four of the ship's seven pools, including the Royal Bay — the largest pool at sea. Cloud 17 adults-only retreat, the Cove Pool with infinity edge, and Swim & Tonic, the cruise line's first swim-up bar at sea.
The Hideaway
Adults-only beach club vibe with a suspended infinity pool, beachside cabanas, and a private atmosphere separate from the rest of the ship. The most serene spot for a sea day.
AquaDome
A futuristic glass dome at the bow of the ship with 220-degree ocean views. Home to the AquaTheater — live high-diving and aerial performances. Also where Panoramic Ocean View staterooms are located.
Central Park
Real trees, live plants, open-air restaurants, and a walkable boulevard through the middle of the ship. Quieter and more relaxed than the Promenade, with good evening dining options and a garden ambiance.
Royal Promenade
The ship's main indoor boulevard — 15 restaurants, bars, and lounges including Bolero's and The Pearl Cafe. Anchored by the Pearl, a 3,000-panel kinetic art installation that's one of the most striking things on the ship.
Surfside
Built specifically for families with young kids — carousel, splash pads, three water experiences, an adults-only pool at The Lime & Coconut bar, arcade, and the Social020 teen club. The most family-dense area of the ship.
Suite Neighborhood
Exclusive to suite guests: private sun deck, Suite Lounge, and access to Coastal Kitchen. A ship-within-a-ship experience with dedicated concierge service and priority embarkation/disembarkation.

Bahamas · Day 2

Perfect Day at CocoCay

7:00 AM – 4:00 PM

Royal Caribbean's private island in the Berry Islands — 125 acres exclusively for guests on your ship. The main beaches, Oasis Lagoon (the largest freshwater pool in the Caribbean), complimentary buffets, and lounge chairs are all included with your fare. Thrill Waterpark and Hideaway Beach (adults-only, with swim-up bar and DJ) cost extra. If you book your drink package onboard, it carries over to the island.

Oasis Lagoon (free) 5 beaches Thrill Waterpark (extra) Hideaway Beach (extra) Drink package transfers

Curaçao · Day 5

Willemstad, Curaçao

10:30 AM – 6:00 PM

The ship docks within half a mile of downtown — no tender, no transfer needed. Willemstad is a UNESCO World Heritage city: think Dutch colonial architecture painted in vivid coral, gold, and turquoise along the Handelskade waterfront, connected by the floating Queen Emma pedestrian bridge. Walk to Fort Amsterdam, the oldest synagogue in the Americas (Mikvé Israel-Emanuel, consecrated 1732), and the Kura Hulanda museum. The Chobolobo distillery produces the original blue Curaçao liqueur on-site. Beaches like Mambo Beach and Kokomo are a short taxi ride away.

Walkable from ship UNESCO waterfront Queen Emma Bridge Blue Curaçao distillery Hato Caves

Aruba · Day 6

Oranjestad, Aruba

7:00 AM – 4:00 PM

Aruba docks you directly in the capital — four berths right at the edge of downtown Oranjestad. The island is 20 miles tip to tip, so day-tripping is realistic. Eagle Beach and Palm Beach are 15–20 minutes by taxi and consistently rank among the best in the Caribbean — wide, white sand, calm turquoise water, and no seaweed. For something more adventurous: UTV tours through Arikok National Park and its desert landscape of cacti, cliffs, and natural bridges. The California Lighthouse and Ayo Rock Formation are also popular stops. Duty-free shopping at the Renaissance Mall and three-story Royal Plaza Mall is right at the port.

Eagle Beach (taxi, 15 min) Palm Beach Arikok National Park California Lighthouse Duty-free shopping
