Hotel Xcaret isn't a conventional all-inclusive — it's closer to a base camp for a week of structured adventure. You land, the shuttle's waiting, and from that point the logistics are handled: meals, drinks, every park on the list, and transportation between all of them. Suite Garden rooms are genuinely spacious for a family of four — you're not on top of each other. The pools are good, the underground river at Xcaret Park is something kids tend to want to do twice, and between seven included parks there is no realistic way to run out of things to do in five days.
