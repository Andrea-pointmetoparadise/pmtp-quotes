The Itinerary

Where You're Going

Seven ports across the Adriatic and Tyrrhenian. You'll walk the 2km walls of Dubrovnik's old city, step inside Diocletian's Palace in Split (he retired there in 305 AD), peer up at Kotor's Bay of Kotor from a tender boat, eat cannoli in the shadow of Mount Etna in Messina, and end with the Amalfi coast draped over the hills above Salerno. Rome on the far end if you want to extend.
